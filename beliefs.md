## The Bible
## We believe the Scriptures are the inspired and authoritative Word of God.

## God
## We believe in one God who exists eternally in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

## Jesus Christ
## We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose again to give us eternal life.

## Salvation
## We believe salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone.

## The Church
## We believe the church is called to worship God, disciple others, fellowship with one another, and be on mission sharing the gospel.

## The Christian Life
## We believe that Christ followers, believers, are called to holiness, love, obedience, and service.
