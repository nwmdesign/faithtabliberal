# We Invite You to Visit Our Church

We would love for you to visit Faith Tabernacle Church! Whether you are looking for a church home, seeking to grow in your faith, or simply want to learn more about our church family, we invite you to join us.

**Faith Tabernacle Church**  
514 N Kansas Ave, 
Liberal, Kansas  

**Sunday School — 9:00 AM**  
**Sunday Worship Service — 10:00 AM**  

## When You Arrive

When you arrive at Faith Tabernacle Church, you will find us located across from the library in the heart of Liberal. Parking is available along the street surrounding the church.

As you enter, you will be welcomed by a friendly church family that is excited to worship together. We invite you to come as you are, find a seat, and join us as we worship God and open His Word together.

## Sunday Worship

Our Sunday services are centered on worshiping God, growing in our faith, and learning from the truth of Scripture. We offer a traditional worship service with Christ-centered music, prayer, and biblical teaching that encourages believers to grow deeper in their understanding of God’s Word.

Through faithful teaching and preaching, our desire is to help each person grow in their relationship with Christ and discover how God’s truth applies to everyday life. Whether you are new to the faith or have been walking with Christ for many years, we welcome you to grow with us as we seek to know God more deeply.

We look forward to worshiping with you!
